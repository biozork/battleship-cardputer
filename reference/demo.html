Cardputer Battleship

Two Cardputers talking straight to each other over ESP-NOW, no router and no pairing. Tap a device or click it and use your keyboard to play it. The middle column shows every message the two devices send.

Radio messages

Each line is one ESP-NOW packet. The real ones are a few bytes each.

    How a game goes

    Both devices search for each other and connect on their own. Each player places five ships: move with ; . , /, rotate with r, place with ok, or press a to place the rest automatically.

    Whoever finishes placing first fires first. Turns alternate. When your partner fires, your screen jumps to your own fleet for a moment so you see where it landed, then back to your shots. tab switches between the two views any time.

    After a game, both press ok for a rematch. The score carries over between games.

    On a computer keyboard, arrow keys and Enter work too.
